# Attribution for the additional DESI analysis

The cframe data and selected-row numerical extracts in this directory use DESI DR1, cited as DESI Collaboration et al. (2026), *Data Release 1 of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument*, AJ 171, 285. The [DESI DR1 release page](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/dr1/) and [license and acknowledgments](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/acknowledgments/) identify the applicable CC BY 4.0 terms.

Changes made here: selection of exact TARGETID/FIBER rows, storage in compressed NumPy arrays, rejection of flagged/nonfinite/nonpositive-IVAR pixels during analysis, fixed camera joins, synthetic photometric recalibration, and colour/shape integration. The saved original-row flux arrays are not photometrically modified. This project is independent of the DESI Collaboration; the conclusions below do not imply DESI endorsement.
