# Follow-up: complete review records, colour identifiability, and public atmosphere models

**19 October 2026 UTC. Project direction: Shota Moriwaki. Exploratory, unreviewed research, including ChatGPT-assisted analysis, verification and writing.** [日本語の詳細報告](FOLLOWUP_2026-10-19.ja.md) · [Previous research report](README.md)

The central result is a concrete distinction between matching photometry and uniquely identifying a spectrum's colour or composition. For J0429−0354, we constructed both green-dominant and red-dominant spectra consistent with the same observed griz error bounds, using maximum relative deformations of approximately **4.25%**. For J0913+4434, simple atmosphere controls reproduce broad-band photometry reasonably well while a published metal-atmosphere fit retains local spectral residuals. These results sharpen the next tests; they do not establish a new star, absorption mechanism, or methodological priority.

We numerically screened all 1,684 plotted entities and additionally viewed all 30 selected continuum-zoom diagnostics. Their display-ratio threshold is an inspection rule, not a preregistered physical significance threshold. [Display diagnostics](outlier_followup/display_diagnostics/visual_review.ja.md)

### Calibration branches created large differences between proven duplicates

Native-pixel matching establishes three MWDD/direct pairs as representations of the **same SDSS observation**. Two pairs have current chromaticity differences of 0.0135968 and 0.0151713 in uv. Applying the same already accepted MWDD correction to both representations reduces those differences to **6.16×10⁻⁷ and 1.45×10⁻⁶**, respectively. No spectrum-to-spectrum fit was performed. The third pair was already uncalibrated on both sides.

Replaying the frozen photometry identifies the branches: one pair falls on opposite sides of the 0.15 mag fit-residual acceptance threshold; the other has PS1 fallback photometry only on the MWDD side. The first pair's direct photometry comes from the original FITS BESTOBJID, but the MWDD photometric ID/epoch is absent, so the imaging identity remains unresolved. The common-calibration figure removes a misleading difference between duplicate representations; it does not validate the correction physically or resolve the remaining XP/DESI differences. ## 4. Matching four bands does not always fix colour dominance

For all 25 available initial candidates, we tested positive multiplicative cubic-spline corrections across **450 settings and 1,800 linear programs**, retaining finite negative input samples. Settings vary knot spacing, gri/griz constraints and multiples of the published band errors.

At 800 Å knot spacing and one published error per band, J0429's minimum maximum deformation for griz consistency is **4.249%**. Two separately constructed solutions, away from the dominance boundary, remain green-dominant and red-dominant within approximately that same budget. J0913 needs **1.068%** for griz consistency and **5.927%** to reach nongreen dominance. Its latter bound changes to approximately 5.14–9.56% across the tested correction spaces.

For the 25 candidates, the griz-consistency minimum has median **2.286%** and maximum **7.637%**; **14/25 cannot satisfy these constraints with a deformation budget of 2% or less**. This is conditional on the specified spline space and error boxes. It does not measure the instrument's actual error distribution, a failure probability, or a population rate. Here z participates in fitting; it is no longer a held-out prediction. Published per-band error boxes are not joint confidence regions, and the existing Swan 0.01 mag term was not added again. [Definitions, contrasting solutions and verification](calibration_followup/README.md)

## 5. Public models provide useful but limited comparisons

We performed **720 fits**: 25 candidates plus 47 SDSS controls, two compositions and five gravities. Public Montréal pure-H/pure-He tables with **2021 headers** were fitted using gri to predict z and y. They are older composition controls, not the updated mixed, metal-rich or ultracool models. J0913's pure-H z/y residuals are −0.026/+0.026 mag; pure-He gives −0.037/+0.017 mag. Broad-band agreement therefore does not uniquely identify composition. Both compositions give |z residual| > 0.05 mag for 23/25 candidates, but many also fit gri poorly. Different control filters and selection prevent a false-positive-rate interpretation. [Numerical fits and model versions](model_validation/README.md)

We also matched all 25 candidates to published Kilic PDF figures by Gaia ID and extracted their plotted observed/model vectors. For J0913, mean residuals normalized to a common 6200–6500 Å reference are **+11.63% at 3850–4350 Å** and **−8.25% at 5700–6100 Å**. These percentages differ from the spline-deformation definition. Limited relative-Fν figures, plotting transformations and prior fitting prevent native full-visible Fλ integration, abundance measurements or independent predictive validation. [All 25 residual records](model_validation/published_curve_residuals.csv)

## 6. Research direction and reproduction

The strongest present paper direction is object-specific colour identifiability under measured calibration constraints. Follow-up hypotheses include freeing metal abundance ratios for J0913, empirically testing whether J0429's contrasting solutions remain permissible, and comparing carbon absorption and updated mixed-atmosphere physics through unused photometry or independent epochs. The general nonuniqueness of spectra under a few band integrals is established background; scientific novelty requires a controlled comparison and successful new predictions. Suitable native DZ/DQ model arrays remain unavailable from the inspected public distributions. [Hypotheses, missing inputs and detailed interpretation](FOLLOWUP_2026-10-19.ja.md)
